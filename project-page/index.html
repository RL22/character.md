The problem

AI image sets break when the face keeps changing.

The image can look polished and still fail. Character.md treats identity as a system before the model gets creative with wardrobe, setting, and mood.

01

Face structure drifts

Jaw, cheeks, eyes, nose, and proportions slowly become a different person.

02

Hair and skin get rewritten

Hairline, beard density, texture, and skin detail shift between outputs.

03

Lifestyle scenes hide identity

Props, hats, wide shots, low light, and side angles make drift harder to catch.
