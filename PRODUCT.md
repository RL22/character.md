# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Character.md serves creators, founders, marketers, AI builders, consultants, and non-technical business owners who want consistent AI-generated identity imagery without learning image tooling, local scripts, or prompt-engineering internals.

The project page serves people evaluating Sprintz as an AI builder and systems partner. They should quickly understand what Character.md does, why it matters, and how the workflow reduces character drift.

## Product Purpose

Character.md is a lightweight agent skill kit for turning uploaded reference images into a reusable character identity, an approved anchor headshot, a multi-angle identity sheet, and final headshot or lifestyle imagery.

The project page should build authority for Sprintz by showing a practical AI workflow, not just describing it. Success means visitors understand the identity-first process, trust the system, and exchange an email address to download the skill kit.

## Brand Personality

Structured, practical, credible, and sharp. The voice should feel like an experienced AI builder explaining a useful system in plain language.

The page should feel simple, premium, polished, and technically credible without becoming generic SaaS, developer documentation, or a loud AI-tool landing page.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic SaaS landing pages, purple AI gradients, glassmorphism, decorative dashboards, Gumroad-style download pages, dense developer docs, and overpromising "magic AI" language.

Avoid presenting Character.md as a plugin, app, SaaS product, Python package, OpenCV workflow, MediaPipe setup, or local model download.

## Design Principles

- Show the system working through visual progression, not abstract claims.
- Keep the workflow approachable for non-technical users.
- Let identity consistency be the hero, with the download gate as the natural next step.
- Use Sprintz restraint: warm, structured, decisive, and conversion-aware.
- Make Sprintz feel like the builder behind a practical AI workflow.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast, visible keyboard focus, clear form labels, semantic structure, and reduced-motion support. Do not rely on color alone for validation or interaction states.
