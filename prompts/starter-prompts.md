# Starter Prompts

Use these prompts after filling out `character.md`.

Replace bracketed text with your details.

---

## 1. Baseline headshot

```txt
[Character DNA]

[Face Lock Snippet]

Wearing [simple neutral outfit], no visible logos.

Clean neutral background, head-and-shoulders portrait, calm focused expression.

85mm lens, f/2.0, eye-level framing, centered composition, sharp focus on eyes.

Soft diffused key light at 45 degrees, subtle fill light, gentle shadow falloff, natural skin tones.

Photorealistic, natural skin texture, consistent identity, no visible brands, no stylization, no artificial beautification.
```
