# Lifestyle Prompts

Lifestyle mode keeps the same Character DNA while adding culture, setting, props, and story.

Use for websites, podcasts, social posts, newsletters, and content campaigns.

Do not start with full lifestyle scenes until an anchor headshot and transition portrait pass identity review.

Every prompt below is a template. Replace each bracketed field with the matching line from the user's own Facial Feature Map and character file. A fully filled-in example is in the "Worked example" section at the end of this file.

Reference priority:

1. Original front, three-quarter, and profile photos are the identity truth.
2. The approved anchor headshot is a quality and style anchor.
3. Lifestyle scene details are flexible and must never override facial structure.

Early lifestyle constraints:

- Face must remain large enough in frame to recognize clearly.
- Use front-facing or slight three-quarter angles first.
- Avoid beanies, headphones, microphones, heavy shadows, strong side profiles, and wide environmental framing until identity is stable.
- Reject any output where the face becomes wider, jaw becomes heavier, beard becomes denser, hairline fills in, skin becomes too smooth, or the subject looks like a different founder/model.

```txt
[Character DNA]

[Face Lock Snippet]

Reference priority: original front, three-quarter, and profile photos define identity; the approved anchor headshot defines quality and realism only.

The subject's face must be clearly visible and occupy a meaningful portion of the frame. Preserve the [face shape and facial silhouette], [hairline trait], eye shape, brow weight, nose profile, mouth width, [chin trait], and [facial hair density] from the reference photos.

Wearing [an outer layer over a base layer from the Wardrobe System], [fit], no hat, no headphones, no visible logos.

Seated in a warm café with natural textures, laptop open, ceramic coffee mug nearby, environment visible but secondary to the face.

70mm lens, f/2.8, slight three-quarter angle, waist-up portrait, face sharp and dominant, subtle background blur.

Natural window light with warm tones, soft shadows, realistic indoor lighting, no heavy shadows across the face.

Photorealistic, consistent identity, natural skin texture, no visible brands, natural candid moment, not a stock photo. Reject if the face becomes broader, jaw becomes heavier, hairline fills in, beard thickens, or the subject looks like a different person.
```
