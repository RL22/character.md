# Headshot Prompts

Headshot mode is for high identity fidelity.

Use it for LinkedIn, profiles, press images, website heroes, and speaker bios.

---

## Clean founder headshot

```txt
[Character DNA]

[Face Lock Snippet]

Wearing a neutral crewneck or knit top, smart casual, no collar, no visible logos.

Clean studio portrait, neutral warm background, calm focused expression, shoulders relaxed, direct eye contact.

85mm lens, f/2.0, eye-level framing, centered composition, sharp focus on eyes.

Soft diffused key light at 45 degrees, subtle fill light, natural shadows, realistic skin texture.

Photorealistic, consistent identity, no over-smoothing, no artificial beautification.
```

## Cinematic founder portrait

```txt
[Character DNA]

[Face Lock Snippet]

Wearing dark minimal layers, smart casual, no visible logos.

Cinematic portrait, calm and thoughtful expression, face still large and clearly recognizable.

70mm lens, f/2.8, eye-level or very slight low angle, subtle rule-of-thirds composition.

Directional key light from side, subtle rim light, controlled contrast, moody but realistic, no heavy shadows across identity-critical facial features.

Photorealistic, consistent identity, natural skin texture, no stylization, no face widening, no heavier jaw, no recasting.
```
